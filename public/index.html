Welcome

Why Even? I want to blog, even if the medium has been thoroughly commodified. Expressing yourself online without a concern for image or money is almost unheard of nowadays, but I need this space to get some thoughts out of my head. The dangers of indulging in a journal too much are evident. It’s unhealthy to have so many thoughts alone, it’ll drive a man mad. So this space is part idea board, part diary, part sanity check, part manifesto canvas, and part whatever else. Just a brief glimpse into my dark and twisted mind as I scream myself hoarse into the void of a billion faceless text boxes. ...
