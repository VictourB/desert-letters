FalloutNV: Gaius Octavian Marcinus

A Fallout New Vegas character sheet for Gaius Octavian, A fragmented legion deserter with burning anger and an unknown destiny.
October 22, 2026 · 2 min · Victour

“Phoenix of Mars”#

He was born amidst smoke and ash, or so it was told to him. As a baby, the Legion took his village. It was some rural backwater in Arizona, unmarked and unimportant.

The few inhabitants resisted and were subsequently killed. The entire ramshackle village was put to the torch. Faintly mixed with the crackle and pop of burning wood, cries from an infant could be heard. When red and orange died down to blacks and charred browns, the voice of young Gaius remained. They dug through the ruins to find him unharmed; A phoenix in the cradle.

He was only a few months old. Caesar himself took the boy in his arms, kissed his forehead, and held him high above the soldiers of Mars. In a great, booming gesture, he declared that the orphan would one day grow up to be a strong and faithful legionnaire. Then named him on the spot.

Gaius Octavian’s earliest memories were in legionary camps, sharing in meals and helping the soldiers prepare for battle. He couldn’t remember a time when he lived apart from his brothers. He knew that Caesar and Mars were always with him. His life had meaning and he grew up strong and capable.

Caesar has gone west, and with him the bulk of the Legion’s forces. The latest reports had him directly at the dam. He wanted to personally oversee all aspects of this offensive. Western expansion must be important. This New California Republic might be responsible. Tales of their dishonor shower the markets and baths.

At 29, Gaius received an order to escort a senator. This senator shared Caesar’s secret, and he is now distraught, unsure of both Caesar and his Legion. He has decided to desert and hitches a ride with a merchant’s caravan heading toward New Vegas. With luck and fortune, he can find his destiny there.
